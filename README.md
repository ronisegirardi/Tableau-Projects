# Global Health Dashboard

## Project Overview

An interactive **Tableau dashboard** developed during a **Data Technician Bootcamp** to analyse global health trends, life expectancy, population growth, and demographic data.

The project transforms complex datasets into clear and engaging visualisations, allowing users to explore how factors such as **BMI, gender, geography, and time** relate to life expectancy and population trends.

## Key Skills & Techniques

### 1. Data Preparation & Analysis

- Cleaned, structured, and aggregated demographic and health data for analysis.
- Worked with geographical and time-based data to enable regional and historical comparisons.
- Prepared data for interactive visualisation and dashboard development.

### 2. Calculated Fields & Custom Metrics

- Created **calculated fields** in Tableau to produce additional metrics.
- Calculated measures such as:
  - Average life expectancy
  - Population growth percentages
  - Average BMI
  - Population distributions
- Used calculated metrics to support meaningful comparisons and analysis.

### 3. Interactive Dashboards, Filters & Slicers

- Created an interactive dashboard allowing users to explore the data dynamically.
- Used **filters and slicers** to control the information displayed.
- Included filters for:
  - Country
  - Gender
  - Continent
  - Year
- Used interactive filtering and dashboard actions so selections in one visual could update other elements of the dashboard.

### 4. Data Visualisation

Used a variety of Tableau visualisations to communicate different aspects of the data:

| Visualisation | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Bar Chart** | Compare life expectancy and population growth between countries and regions |
| **Line Chart** | Show changes in life expectancy over time |
| **Pie Chart** | Visualise population distribution by gender |
| **Map** | Explore geographical differences in health and demographic indicators |
| **Scatter Plot** | Explore the relationship between BMI and life expectancy |
| **Diverging Bar Chart** | Compare positive and negative population growth |

## Data Storytelling & Insights

The project demonstrates how data visualisation can turn large and complex datasets into an understandable **data story**.

The dashboard allows users to identify patterns and differences between regions, explore changes over time, and investigate relationships between health and demographic indicators.

Examples include:

- Comparing life expectancy between continents.
- Exploring population growth across different countries.
- Identifying changes in life expectancy over time.
- Examining differences in population by gender.
- Exploring the relationship between **BMI and life expectancy**.
- Using geographical visualisations to identify regional patterns.

## Tech Stack

- **Tableau Desktop** — Data analysis, calculated fields, visualisation and dashboard development.
- **Tableau Public** — Publishing and sharing the interactive dashboard.
- **Global Health & Demographics Dataset** — Source data containing health, population and demographic indicators.

## Key Takeaways

- Developed an **interactive Tableau dashboard** from raw datasets.
- Created calculated fields and custom metrics for analysis.
- Used **filters, slicers and dashboard interactions** to enable user-driven exploration.
- Applied a variety of chart types to communicate different data insights.
- Used **data storytelling** to transform numerical information into clear visual narratives.
- Developed visualisations to explore **trends, comparisons, demographics and performance indicators**.

This project was completed as part of a **Level 3 Data Technician Bootcamp** and demonstrates practical experience in data analysis and visualisation using Tableau.

It showcases the ability to take a dataset, analyse its key characteristics, create meaningful metrics, and communicate findings through an **interactive dashboard and visual data story**.
